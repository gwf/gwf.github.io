## A Little Give and Take

To date, I have received a couple of emails from people starting up classes based on the book (even a high school class---very cool). Would you like to exchange ideas for class activities, projects, lectures, and experiments? If so, this is the place to do it. Send me [email](mailto:gary.flake@usa.net) with any questions that your have, or with resources that you want to share.

Possible contributions could be:

-  lecture transparencies in power-point, PS, or PDF,
-  a compilation of student project ideas,
-  problem sets and solutions within specific areas,
-  quizzes, tests, homework or program assignments,
-  actual student projects, or
-  source code for new and improved examples.

So, in a nutshell, if you have something to contribute, [send it to me](mailto:gary.flake@usa.net). If you need something and want to post a request, [send that as well](mailto:gary.flake@usa.net).

This page will have a download section for class / instructor resources as soon as critical mass on contributions is achieved,

## Recent *CBofN* Sightings

The following is a partial list of classes that use (or have used) *CBofN*.

- [Stephanie Forrest](http://www.cs.unm.edu/~forrest/) of the [University of New Mexico](http://www.unm.edu/) is teaching Topics in Complex Adaptive Systems with *CBofN*.

- [Hayward Alker](http://www.usc.edu/dept/LAS/ir/faculty/tr-alker.htm) of the [University of Southern California](http://www.usc.edu/) taught an advanced International Relations course based on *CBofN*.

- [Michael Schmitt](http://www.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/lmi/mschmitt/) of Ruhr-Universität Bochum teaches [Algorithmic Description of Complex Systems](http://www.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/ffm/sommer/300.htm) and uses *CBofN* as the primary text.

- [Sam Samanta](http://snyflcaa.fingerlakes.edu/~SAMANTPG/) of [Finger Lakes Community College](http://www.fingerlakes.edu/) uses *CBofN* as the primary text in his course entitled [CHAOS: The Self Organizing Universe](http://www.sln.suny.edu/ADMIN/SLN/catalog/slncat.nsf/7002dad410c84b91852568b700528f88/c61e2ed17a4d4f0e852568b90048a535?OpenDocument).

- [Douglas White](http://eclectic.ss.uci.edu/~drwhite/) of the [University of California Irvine](http://www.uci.edu/) uses *CBofN* as a recommended and collateral reading in his [Social Dynamics and Self Organizing Systems](http://eclectic.ss.uci.edu/~drwhite/Anthro179a/syllabus.htm) course.

- [David P. Feldman](http://hornacek.coa.edu/dave/index.html) of the [College of the Atlantic](http://www.coa.edu/) teaches a course on [Chaotic Dynamics](http://hornacek.coa.edu/dave/Teaching/Chaotic_Dynamics/index.html) and uses *CBofN* as a supplementary text.

- [Mark White](http://white.ece.ncsu.edu/) of [North Carolina State University](http://www.ncsu.edu/) uses *CBofN* in his [Distributed Computation](http://white.ece.ncsu.edu/ECE791N/) course.

- [Dave Goforth](http://www.cs.laurentian.ca/dgoforth/home.html) of [Laurentian University](http://www.cs.laurentian.ca/) has plans to use part five of *CBofN* in his [Artificial Intelligence](http://www.cs.laurentian.ca/dgoforth/cosc4117/outline.html) course.

- [Thiemo Krink](http://www.daimi.au.dk/~krink/) of the [University of Aarhus](http://www.au.dk/), Denmark, uses *CBofN* in his [EvALife Seminar](http://www.evalife.dk/seminar_fall_1999/index.html), “Multiagent systems in interdisciplinary research on information technology and life sciences”.

- [David Mullins](http://www.sar.usf.edu/~ncmath/dave.html) of the [New College](http://www.newcollege.usf.edu/) of the University of South Florida used *CBofN* for a student directed self-study course on artificial life and complex systems.
