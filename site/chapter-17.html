The Spatial Prisoner's Dilemma (sipd.c (and eipd.c))

Every cell on the grid plays the iterated Prisoner's Dilemma against its eight neighbors using one of five strategies (always cooperate, always defect, random, tit-for-tat, Pavlov), then copies whichever neighbor scored best. Cooperators survive by clustering; defectors invade the clusters and then starve. A little noise keeps the map churning between tit-for-tat and Pavlov with blooms of defection. Full screen →
