Diffusion-Limited Aggregation (diffuse.c)

Particles wander at random until they touch the growing cluster, then freeze. Because a wandering particle is far more likely to bump into a tip than to find its way into a crevice, the cluster grows branches, and the branches grow branches. The result has a fractional dimension of about 1.7, the same as coral, frost, and electrical discharge. Runs alternate between one seed, several seeds, and a line seed along the bottom that grows a forest. Full screen →
